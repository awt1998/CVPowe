# CVPower — Vision

## The mission
Build the **best free resume optimization platform** for job seekers. Not another resume builder — an optimization engine whose only job is to increase a candidate's chance of getting an interview.

## What makes CVPower different
Three commitments, each a headline feature rather than a footnote:

1. **It optimizes, it doesn't just format.** Every feature must measurably move the match score or the interview odds. A prettier template that doesn't help you get hired is out of scope.
2. **It's private by architecture, not by policy.** No backend, no database, no accounts, no tracking. Your resume and the job description never leave your browser. We can make that promise because the code makes it impossible to break.
3. **It's free forever.** No paywalls, no feature gating, no "pro" tier. Sustainability comes from being open-source, not from monetizing desperation.

## Who it's for
People trying to get a job who are underserved by the current market: career switchers who don't know how to reframe their experience, new grads padding thin resumes, privacy-conscious professionals who refuse to upload their CV to a SaaS, and multilingual applicants who need Arabic/English and RTL support.

## The feeling
CVPower should feel like a premium commercial product — the polish of Apple, the precision of Linear, the calm of Notion, the trust of Stripe, the speed of Vercel — while being completely free and open. Whitespace, typography, and clarity over decoration.

## The north star
A candidate pastes their resume and a job description, sees exactly why they match and what to fix, applies the fixes, watches the score climb, and exports an ATS-safe PDF — in one sitting, on their own device, with nothing uploaded.
